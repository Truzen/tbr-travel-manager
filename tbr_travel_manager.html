📖 Help & Documentation

Getting Started

  1. Click "Sign In" to authenticate with your work account
  2. Select the month you're submitting for
  3. Add each trip with start/end locations
  4. Select M&I and Lodging options
  5. Attach receipts/invoices (optional)
  6. Click "Add Trip" to save
  7. When done, click "Submit to SharePoint"

Understanding The Form

  • Date: When you traveled
  • From/To: Starting and ending locations (distance auto-calculated)
  • M&I Options:
    • Full Day: $59 (full day of meals)
    • Partial: $44.25 (only breakfast or dinner)
    • Custom: Enter your actual amount
  • Lodging:
    • GSA: Government standard rate
    • Custom: Your actual hotel cost
    • None: No hotel needed

Attachments

Drag hotel invoices, meal receipts, or parking receipts into the attachment box.

  • Supports: PDF, JPG, PNG
  • Max 5MB per file
  • Multiple files per trip

Exporting Your Data

  • Export PDF: Professional report for submission
  • Export CSV: Spreadsheet format for backup
  • Copy All: Text format for email/sharing

Troubleshooting

  • Can't sign in? Try a different browser or incognito window
  • Distance not calculating? Check address spelling
  • Lost your data? Your data is saved locally. If you cleared your browser cache, it may be gone. Always export PDF!
  • Still have issues? See the complete documentation or contact your administrator

Important Notes

  • Your data is auto-saved to your browser (survives browser close)
  • Month selection is manual to prevent submitting for wrong month
  • Always export your PDF before clearing browser data
  • Your name auto-detects from your work account (Last, First format)
  • Submitted files go to: /[Month]/[Your Name]/ in SharePoint
